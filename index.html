About

Although Canyonlands hails from Nashville, their sound is anything but pop country. Influenced by artists such as John Denver, Train, John Mayer, and the Rolling Stones, Canyonlands brings it all together for the perfect blend of Americana soft rock.

Jack Stein (guitar, BGVs) and Melissa Findley (lead vocals) collaborate to create what makes Canyonlands’ music so special: beautiful melodies with haunting harmonies, accompanied by creative acoustic guitar leads that will leave listeners wanting more.
